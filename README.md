# TitanArch - Construction, Renovation & Space Management

A professional website for TitanArch, a leading construction, renovation, and space management services company based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

## About TitanArch

TitanArch specializes in providing comprehensive construction solutions including:
- **Construction Services** - Complete building construction from foundation to finishing
- **Renovation Projects** - Residential and commercial property renovations
- **Space Management** - Efficient space planning and optimization solutions
- **Architectural Design** - Creative and functional architectural designs
- **Project Management** - End-to-end project coordination and execution

## Features

- Responsive design for all devices (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- Service showcase with detailed descriptions
- Project portfolio gallery
- Client testimonials section
- Contact form with inquiry management
- About us and team information
- Service area coverage details
- Real-time project cost calculator
